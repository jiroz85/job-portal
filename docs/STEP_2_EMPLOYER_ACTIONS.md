## 💡 **Pro Tips for Employers:**

### **Best Practices:**

1. ⚡ **Respond quickly** - Within 24 hours
2. 📧 **Personalize messages** - Use applicant's name
3. 📅 **Offer flexibility** - Multiple interview slots
4. 📝 **Add notes** - Remember key details
5. 🔄 **Keep updates** - Regular status changes

### **Common Mistakes to Avoid:**

1. ❌ **Don't leave applications pending** too long
2. ❌ **Don't send generic messages**
3. ❌ **Don't forget to update status**
4. ❌ **Don't skip the interview scheduling**
5. ❌ **Don't lose applicant communication**
